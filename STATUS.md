# TinyTrip status

_Last updated: 2026-11-14 ("Next" not yet confirmed by DK)_

## Goal
A calm, step-by-step guide for first-time parents planning a first trip with a baby (0–2). Launch as a real
product; the aim for now is helping parents, not revenue. Planned retention feature: saved, personalised trip
plans per account.

## Stage
Pre-launch. Static Astro site on Netlify; trip saved in the browser only (no accounts yet). 10 pages.
